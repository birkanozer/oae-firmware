OAE Robin E3 — Ender 3 stock LCD (ENH12864Z-1, ST7920, 128×64) mockups, shown ×4

Each screen is a real 128×64 one-bit bitmap: layout, cell sizes and inversions are exact for the U8g2 fonts named in the captions (5×7, 6×10, 7×13 bold, 10×20).
